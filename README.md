## 🚀 What I'm Building

> Most of these repos are private. Click any project to see more on nesturex.com.

### 📊 [Bet Ledger](https://nesturex.com/betledger) `live on Play Store`
A bet tracking app for Kenyan sports bettors. Log your bets, see your real numbers, and get honest insights on what's working and what isn't.

`React Native` `Expo` `Firebase` `Google Sign-In`

[![Get it on Google Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get_it_on-Google_Play-1a6fd4?style=for-the-badge&logo=googleplay&logoColor=white&labelColor=0a0a0a)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nesture.betledger)

---

### 🎮 [Solo Standoff](https://nesturex.com/solo-standoff) `in development`
The first title from **NX Gaming**. A mobile tap-to-fire grid puzzle: every character fires when tapped, and the order of your shots is the puzzle. 70 handcrafted levels, a props tray (mirrors, walls, barrels, portals) and weekend Daily Challenges.

`React Native` `Supabase` `AdMob` `Game Design`

---

### 🚐 [Matatu Dash](https://nesturex.com/matatu-dash) `in development`
A Crossy Road style hopper set on Kenyan streets. Dodge matatus, bodabodas and hawkers lane by lane, with real street-recorded audio bringing Nairobi to life. Leaderboards included.

`React Native` `Skia` `Reanimated` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `Game Dev`

---

### 💰 [SoraPesa](https://nesturex.com/sorapesa) `in development`
A Kenya-first money companion for young earners. Import your M-Pesa statement, get every transaction categorised, and finally see where your salary went. Built for side hustlers too, with a module for tracking business revenue, costs and profit.

`Expo` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Edge Functions` `Next.js`

---

### 🏋️ [Kikota](https://nesturex.com/kikota) `MVP complete`
*The operating system for African gyms.* Multi-tenant gym management SaaS with M-Pesa STK Push payments, QR check-ins, a live owner dashboard and full subscription lifecycle. Multi-branch ready from day one.

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Socket.io` `M-Pesa Daraja`
